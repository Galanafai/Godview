* If you are running on the RunPod pod for Phase C (V2V4Real replay), your
  brief is `docs/PHASE_C_HANDOFF.md`. Read it before doing anything.

  dashes or double hyphens, every claim backed by something measured or
  checked, and say what is not proven.
